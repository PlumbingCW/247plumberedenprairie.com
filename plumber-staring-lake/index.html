Plumber near Staring Lake, Eden Prairie, MN

Homes around Staring Lake and its large park sit near water and wetlands. That makes sump pumps, floor drains and basement moisture worth a look alongside the usual fixtures and water heaters.

Staring Lake is one of Eden Prairie's best-known lakes. Local reporting puts it at about 164 acres with a maximum depth of 14 feet, and the surrounding park covers roughly 146 acres. The City lists Staring Lake Park at 14800 Pioneer Trail, the Outdoor Center at 13765 Staring Lake Parkway and Oak Point School at 13400 Staring Lake Parkway. Local real-estate guides use "Staring Lake" as a loose neighborhood name for the homes near those streets.

No official boundary defines the Staring Lake area, so this page does not draw one. If your address is on or near Staring Lake Parkway or Pioneer Trail, or you describe your home as "by Staring Lake," you are in the area this page means. The county also records a subdivision called Staring Lake Village Homes, which suggests townhome-style housing nearby.

Homes near water and wetlands

One local article counts more than 4,500 acres of open space and wetlands and views of 17 lakes in the city. Land near lakes and wetlands can hold more groundwater than higher ground, but conditions differ from lot to lot, so we do not claim any particular address has a problem. What you can do is test the equipment you depend on before a storm.

Signs worth checking near a lake

A sump pump that runs on dry days

Constant running can mean a stuck float, a failing check valve or high groundwater.

Damp walls or a musty smell

Moisture can come from outside water, a plumbing leak or condensation, and the source matters.

A floor drain that gurgles

This can mean a partial blockage in the line or a drain trap that has dried out.

If the pump is old or noisy, sump pump repair covers testing, float adjustment and replacement. A battery backup is worth asking about because storms that raise groundwater are often the same storms that cut power. For recurring dampness, see basement waterproofing, and keep downspouts pointed away from the foundation.

Everyday plumbing, same as anywhere in the city

Most calls in any neighborhood are routine. A slow kitchen drain, a running toilet and a water heater that no longer keeps up are all common.

The City says most homes in Eden Prairie were built under newer plumbing code safeguards, and its water is softened to about 5 grains per gallon, which slows scale. A tank heater still collects sediment, so flushing it and inspecting the anode rod help it last. Our pages on drain cleaning and toilet repair explain the usual fixes.

Seasonal notes for lakeside homes

In spring, snowmelt and rain test a sump pump for the first time since fall. In winter, exterior spigots and lake-side garages can freeze, so disconnect hoses and shut off exterior lines before the first hard freeze. If a pipe freezes, open the nearest faucet and call before it splits.
