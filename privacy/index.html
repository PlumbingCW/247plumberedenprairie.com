Privacy Policy

How Eden Prairie 24/7 Plumbing handles information from phone calls and visits to this website.

24/7 emergency line(952) 866-9120Call now

This policy explains what information we collect when you call our dispatch line or visit this website, and how we use it. This website has no contact forms. Most contact happens by phone.

Information we collect

When you call (952) 866-9120, we may collect your phone number, name, address and a description of your plumbing problem so a plumber can help you. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you visit the website, our hosting provider and standard web tools may log technical data such as browser type, pages visited and approximate location from your network address.

How we use information

We use call information to schedule and complete plumbing work, to answer questions about it, and to improve the quality of our service. We use website data to keep the site working and to understand which pages are useful.

Sharing

We share your information with the plumber who is assigned to your job, and with service providers who help us run the phone line and the website.

Cookies and third parties

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays maps from Google Maps. Those services may set cookies or log your visit under their own policies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@247plumberedenprairie.com or call (952) 866-9120. We keep call records only as long as needed for service, quality and legal reasons.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the bottom of this page shows the latest revision.

Last updated: October 16, 2026.
