How to choose the right service page

Start with the symptom. Water where it should not be belongs with leak detection or burst pipe repair. A drain that will not clear starts with drain cleaning, and a toilet gurgling while the washer drains points to sewer line cleaning. A cold shower belongs with water heater repair.

If more than one fixture is affected, assume the problem is shared. A single slow sink is a branch problem, while three slow drains at once usually mean a main line. When the problem is shared, say so on the phone, because it changes what the plumber brings.
