Round Lake is a small lake with a large role in Eden Prairie. The City lists Round Lake Park at 16691 Valley View Road. Local reporting says the lake is about 32 acres with a maximum depth of 37 feet, and the park covers about 23 acres. The land was a private gun club and farm until the 1960s, when the City began acquiring it, and the park has hosted the annual Fourth of July celebration starting in 1984.

People use "Round Lake" to describe the homes near the park. The county also records a subdivision called Round Lake Estates, but we do not know its exact boundaries, so this page uses Valley View Road and the park as anchors instead. The Eden Prairie Community Center at 16700 Valley View Road and Eden Prairie High School at 17185 Valley View Road are close neighbors of the park.

## What a plumber looks at in a family household
The 2020 census found that about a third of Eden Prairie households have children under 18. Families put steady demand on a house, and the problems are usually practical ones. Toys and wipes in toilets, grease in kitchen drains and a water heater that runs out of hot water during a busy morning are everyday issues, not signs of a bad neighborhood.

:::cards
### Toilets and drains
Repeated clogs in one fixture usually mean a local blockage, while several fixtures backing up together point to the main line.
### Water heaters
A tank that no longer keeps up, makes popping sounds or leaks at the base needs attention.
### Fixtures and disposals
Dripping faucets, loose handles and jammed disposals are quick fixes when caught early.
:::

Start with simple steps. Use a plunger on a single clogged toilet, avoid chemical drain cleaners before a plumber looks, and never pour grease down a drain. If a clog returns, [[drain-cleaning|drain cleaning]] explains snaking and jetting. For a fixture that leaks or runs, see [[toilet-repair|toilet repair]] and [[faucet-repair|faucet repair]]. If a disposal jams, [[garbage-disposal|garbage disposal]] covers the common causes.

## Water heaters and water quality
Eden Prairie says its supply is softened to about 5 grains per gallon, so scale is slower here than in towns with unsoftened water. A tank heater still collects sediment, and the City mentions that an aging heater can contribute to odors in the water. If yours is more than a decade old, ask a plumber to inspect it before it fails at an inconvenient time. [[water-heater-repair|Water heater repair]] and [[water-heater-installation|water heater installation]] describe the options.

## A note on older and newer homes
The City says most Eden Prairie homes were built after plumbing code safeguards were adopted. We do not know the age of every house near Round Lake, and age alone does not prove a problem. If you want to know what is behind your walls, a [[sewer-inspection|sewer inspection]] or leak check gives facts rather than guesses.

:::faq
Q: Is Round Lake a neighborhood or just a park?
A: Both are used. Round Lake Park is the City's name for the park, and residents also use the name for nearby homes. There is no official neighborhood boundary.

Q: Do you serve homes near the Community Center and the high school?
A: Yes. Those sit on Valley View Road in Eden Prairie, and homes in that area are part of this service area.

Q: What should I do about a toilet that keeps clogging?
A: Check what is being flushed, use a plunger and call if it repeats. Repeated clogs can point to a blockage past the trap.

Q: Is my water heater too old?
A: There is no single age. Look for leaks, rust-colored water, popping noises or lukewarm output, and have a plumber inspect it.
:::
