## Eden Prairie ZIP codes
The city itself uses three ZIP codes: 55344, 55346 and 55347. All three get the same 24/7 emergency line, and each service page explains what to do while you wait. Call [[call|(952) 866-9120]] at any hour, or start with [[drain-cleaning|drain cleaning]], [[water-heater-repair|water heater repair]] or [[leak-detection|leak detection]] if you are not sure where to begin.

## Other places we take calls from
Edina and Chaska callers are welcome on the same line, and the dispatcher will confirm the appointment window for your address. Excelsior, Victoria, Shorewood and Shakopee are covered case by case, since distance and traffic change the timing.

If your town is not on this list, [[call|call anyway]]. Dispatch will tell you honestly whether a plumber can reach you, and it is better to ask than to lose time searching during an emergency. Local water facts differ between towns, which is why each town page spells out what its city says about hardness, wells and meters.
