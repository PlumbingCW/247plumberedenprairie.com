## What to tell the dispatcher
Give your address and the nearest cross street, then describe what you see. Say where the main shutoff is, whether the water is clear or dirty, whether it is near electrical equipment, and whether more than one fixture is affected. A short, clear description saves time because it tells the plumber what to bring.

## While you wait for the plumber
For a burst pipe or active leak, close the main shutoff valve, usually near the water meter in the basement. For a backing-up drain, stop running water in the house. For a gas smell, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. More detail lives on the [[burst-pipe-repair|burst pipe]] and [[sewer-line-cleaning|sewer backup]] pages.
