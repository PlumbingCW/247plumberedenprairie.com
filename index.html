Eden Prairie plumbing, locally

What Eden Prairie homeowners should know before a plumbing emergency

Eden Prairie grew from about 2,000 residents in 1960 to almost 60,000 by 2000, according to the City, with Interstate 494 and Eden Prairie Center arriving in the mid-1970s. That means most homes date from the 1970s onward, so many plumbing problems here come from fixtures, water heaters and sump pumps wearing out rather than from very old pipe.

The City also says its tap water is softened at the treatment plant to about 5 grains per gallon, down from roughly 21 in the well water. For homeowners that means scale builds up slowly, so a water heater that fails early is more likely a sediment, anode or fitting problem than a hard-water one. Our water heater repair page covers the checks.

Your water meter sits inside the house, usually in the basement near the water heater, and the City says it will look into suspected meter problems at no charge. Knowing where that meter and its two shutoff valves are is the fastest way to stop a burst pipe, and it is also where a hidden leak shows up first. We use the same spot for leak detection.

Our dispatch address is 7500 Flying Cloud Dr, Eden Prairie, MN 55344, and calls from the 55344, 55346 and 55347 ZIP codes get the same 24/7 line. Landmarks like Flying Cloud Airport, Staring Lake and Purgatory Creek Park help dispatchers pin down a neighborhood when you are not sure of the cross street.
