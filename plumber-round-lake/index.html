Plumber near Round Lake, Eden Prairie, MN

The Round Lake area centers on a busy community park and the Valley View Road corridor in Eden Prairie. These are everyday family households, so most calls involve drains, fixtures and water heaters.

Round Lake is a small lake with a large role in Eden Prairie. The City lists Round Lake Park at 16691 Valley View Road. Local reporting says the lake is about 32 acres with a maximum depth of 37 feet, and the park covers about 23 acres. The land was a private gun club and farm until the 1960s, when the City began acquiring it, and the park has hosted the annual Fourth of July celebration starting in 1984.

People use "Round Lake" to describe the homes near the park. The county also records a subdivision called Round Lake Estates, but we do not know its exact boundaries, so this page uses Valley View Road and the park as anchors instead. The Eden Prairie Community Center at 16700 Valley View Road and Eden Prairie High School at 17185 Valley View Road are close neighbors of the park.

What a plumber looks at in a family household

The 2020 census found that about a third of Eden Prairie households have children under 18. Families put steady demand on a house, and the problems are usually practical ones. Toys and wipes in toilets, grease in kitchen drains and a water heater that runs out of hot water during a busy morning are everyday issues, not signs of a bad neighborhood.

Toilets and drains

Repeated clogs in one fixture usually mean a local blockage, while several fixtures backing up together point to the main line.

Water heaters

A tank that no longer keeps up, makes popping sounds or leaks at the base needs attention.

Fixtures and disposals

Dripping faucets, loose handles and jammed disposals are quick fixes when caught early.

Start with simple steps. Use a plunger on a single clogged toilet, avoid chemical drain cleaners before a plumber looks, and never pour grease down a drain. If a clog returns, drain cleaning explains snaking and jetting. For a fixture that leaks or runs, see toilet repair and faucet repair. If a disposal jams, garbage disposal covers the common causes.

Water heaters and water quality

Eden Prairie says its supply is softened to about 5 grains per gallon, so scale is slower here than in towns with unsoftened water. A tank heater still collects sediment, and the City mentions that an aging heater can contribute to odors in the water. If yours is more than a decade old, ask a plumber to inspect it before it fails at an inconvenient time. Water heater repair and water heater installation describe the options.
